# geodev-lab-project

GIS project on flood exposure in Obio/Akpor Local Government Area, Rivers State, Nigeria.

## Research Question

Which areas of Obio/Akpor Local Government Area are most exposed to flooding in relation to drainage conditions, elevation, and surface-water patterns?

## Month 01 Work

### Week 1 — Project Brief
The project question, datasets, data sources, and proposed GIS output are documented in the [project brief](project-brief.md).

### Week 2 — Data Notes
The downloaded datasets, sources, key fields, geometry, and data limitations are documented in the [data notes](data-notes.md).

### Week 3 — Prepared Data
The project data were prepared in the projected working CRS, clipped to the study area, and saved as an analysis-ready GeoPackage:

[Analysis-ready GeoPackage](obio_akpor_analysis_ready.gpkg)

### Week 4 — Spatial Analysis
A 100 m buffer was created around the mapped river feature in the projected CRS. The output contains one feature and no empty geometry was found.

[Week 4 map — 100 m River Buffer](week4_river_buffer_map.png)

[Month 1 summary](month-1-summary.md)

## Result

The Week 4 analysis identified the mapped river corridor and its 100 m buffer as an area of proximity to surface water within Obio/Akpor LGA. Further analysis is still needed to incorporate elevation, drainage conditions, and broader surface-water patterns into the flooding exposure assessment.
