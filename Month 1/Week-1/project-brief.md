# My project brief

## The Question

Which areas of Obio/Akpor Local Government Area are most exposed to flooding in relation to drainage conditions, elevation, and surface-water patterns?
## Why It Matters

Flooding can disrupt movement, damage property, and affect communities within Obio/Akpor Local Government Area. Understanding how drainage conditions, elevation, and surface-water patterns relate to flood exposure can help identify areas that may require improved drainage infrastructure and better flood-management planning.

## The Data I Need

1. Administrative boundary: Obio/Akpor Local Government Area boundary to define the study area.

2. Drainage network: Mapped drainage channels and related water-management features.

3. Surface-water history: 30-metre satellite-derived data showing the historical occurrence, recurrence, seasonality, and maximum extent of surface water.

4. Elevation: 30-metre Copernicus GLO-30 Digital Surface Model (DSM) to identify low-lying areas and terrain characteristics.

5. Waterways: Rivers, streams, and other mapped surface-water features.

6. Road network: Street and road data to identify areas and roads potentially affected by flooding.
7. Settlement extents: Mapped settlement areas within Obio/Akpor Local Government Area to identify populated areas potentially exposed to flooding.

 ## Where Each Dataset Comes From
 1. Administrative boundary — GRID3 NGA – Operational LGA Boundaries (December 2020): https://grid3.org/geospatial-data-nigeria
 2. Drainage network — OpenStreetMap (OSM) drainage features, including mapped drains and ditches, accessed through QuickOSM in QGIS: https://www.openstreetmap.org/
 3. Surface-water history — JRC Global Surface Water Mapping Layers v1.4 (30 m): https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/JRC_GSW1_4_GlobalSurfaceWater
 4. Elevation — Copernicus GLO-30 (30 m) Digital Surface Model (DSM): https://portal.opentopography.org/datasetMetadata.jsp?otCollectionID=OT.032021.4326.1
 5. Waterways — OpenStreetMap (OSM) waterways, including mapped rivers and streams, accessed through QuickOSM in QGIS: https://www.openstreetmap.org/
 6. Road network — OpenStreetMap (OSM) road features, including mapped streets and roads, accessed through QuickOSM in QGIS: https://www.openstreetmap.org/
 7. Settlement extents — GRID3 NGA – Settlement Extents v4.1 (August 2026): https://grid3.org/geospatial-data-nigeria

 ## What You Would Build
 I would build a GIS-based flood exposure map for Obio/Akpor Local Government Area. The map would combine elevation, drainage networks, waterways, historical surface-water patterns, settlements, and roads to identify areas with higher potential exposure to flooding. The final map would help show where flood exposure is concentrated and support better flood-management planning.
